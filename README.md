## 🌍 Why East Africa?

East Africa is one of the fastest-growing digital markets on the planet. Most data products are built *for* the West, not *by* or *for* us. I build tools that:

- 📊 **Democratise financial data** — crypto, stocks, and economic news accessible to all
- 🏛️ **Increase government accountability** — tracking public procurement and county spending
- 📰 **Aggregate local news** — with real sentiment analysis and topic filtering
- ⚽ **Power sports intelligence** — predictions, live scores, analytics for local football
- 🎵 **Preserve culture** — digitising vernacular and Kiswahili hymn collections
